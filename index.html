What I do

Most small studios and startups hit the same wall. Production is bottlenecked somewhere, testing is either missing or eating the schedule, and nobody has time to step back and work out what the operation actually needs.

That is the work I do. I evaluate QA strategy, untangle production issues, and help you decide what to build, what to buy, and what to skip. When the right answer is a custom tool or a small app, I build it. When the right answer is a process change and no new software, I will tell you that too.

Who you are working with

I come from years of hands-on QA and IT with a heavy lean toward art and content pipelines, a combination most consultants don't have. I have shipped tools, owned test strategy, and sat inside production long enough to know where the time actually goes.

I use AI where it genuinely shortens the path. A lot of teams are using it to create more work for themselves right now, and part of my job is making sure you are not one of them.
